# Workshop: planning

**Purpose:** close the workshop series: play back what was decided, agree the order of the stages and the first slice, and leave with owners and dates for what's next.
**Length:** 90 minutes · **Unblocks:** the start of the build.
**Participants:** sponsor · product owner · development lead · solution architect · operations lead · facilitator · scribe.

## Prepare
- The assessment's roadmap, updated with every workshop's decisions: the stages, their main items, rough effort and dependencies.
- The open questions still open, each with its owner.

## Agenda
| Time | Block | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Play back | The decisions from each workshop, in two minutes each. Corrections now. |
| 0:20 | The stages | Walk the roadmap; agree the order (stages 4-6 can overlap once the first slice works) and what's out of scope for now. |
| 0:40 | The first slice | Confirm it, and what "done" looks like for it. |
| 0:55 | Open questions | Each one: decide now, or confirm owner and date. |
| 1:10 | Who does what next | Human items (app registrations, consent, sandboxes, subscriptions) with owners; the cadence for acceptance checks and feedback sessions. |
| 1:25 | Close | The sponsor confirms the plan. |

## Decisions to reach
- The order of the stages, and what's out of scope.
- The first slice.
- Owners and dates for the open questions and human items.
- The cadence of acceptance checks and feedback sessions.

## Outputs
TASKS.md › Milestones and the items for the first two stages, through `procedures/roadmap.md`; human items with owners; PRD › Open Questions updated.
