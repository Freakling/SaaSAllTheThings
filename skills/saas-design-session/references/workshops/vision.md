# Workshop: vision and business model

**Purpose:** agree why the product exists as a SaaS, for whom, how it earns, and what it must never become.
**Length:** 2 hours · **Unblocks:** every other workshop.
**Participants:** sponsor (decides budget and scope) · product owner (decides the product) · sales or customer success · a representative of the first customer, if there is one · solution architect · facilitator · scribe.

## Prepare
- From the assessment: what the app does today, who uses it, and the readiness summary.
- A draft pitch and two or three possible plan models (per user, per company, by usage), as options to react to.

## Agenda
| Time | Block | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Frame | The goal of the day and the decisions to reach. Parking lot rules. |
| 0:10 | Why SaaS, why now | Each participant writes the one outcome they want in a year; cluster and name the top three. |
| 0:30 | The pitch | Write the product's pitch as one paragraph together, from the draft: who it's for, the problem, why it beats what they do today. |
| 0:50 | Customers and tenants | Who the tenants are (the kind of organisation), the first one, and the next likely ones. What makes one a good fit. |
| 1:05 | Break | |
| 1:15 | Plans | The plan models as options with a recommendation; decide the model, and list what each plan includes. Prices and limits may stay open. |
| 1:35 | Product pillars | 3–5 statements every later choice is checked against; dot-vote from a longlist. |
| 1:50 | Constraints and close | Deadline, budget, compliance, the customer's Azure governance. Read back the decisions. |

## Decisions to reach
- The pitch, and the first customer.
- Who the tenants are, and what a tenant is (a company, a department, a site).
- The plan model, and what each plan includes.
- 3–5 product pillars.
- The constraints the roadmap must respect.

## Methods
- **Outcome cards:** silent writing first, then clustering, so the loudest voice doesn't set the goal.
- **Dot voting** for the pillars: three dots each, then the owner confirms.

## Outputs
PRD › Pitch, Product Pillars, Customers and Tenants; a line in `product/decisions.md` per decision. Prices and limits that stay open become open questions.
